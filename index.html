04 · About

I start with the person, not the feature.

I talk to customers, sit in on sales calls, and get engineers, partners and stakeholders on the same page before anything is built. I've turned messy problems into something clear enough to build, across product stages and very different fields: the people and the problem matter most to me. Away from work I make gifts and pictures by hand, which is probably why I sweat the details.
