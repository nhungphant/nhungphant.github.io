Edupia

Kids try the app before parents buy

Let the lessons make the case.

Year
2023
Company
Educa Corporation
Role
Product Owner
Team
4 people

Edupia is an English self-learning app for Vietnamese children aged 5 to 11. I built a free trial so parents could see their child learn before any sales call, instead of waiting by the phone.

Get the appApp Store ↗Google Play ↗

01Outcome

New users now try the app themselves first, and come to the sales call already knowing whether it fits their child. Proved in an A/B test, then rolled out to every new user.

+19%orders per lead
+24%new users finishing lesson 1 on their first day

02The problem

Activation depended on the Sales team, who phoned every number that signed up.

Parents took the time to sign up, then got nothing back. To learn about Edupia, they had to search for it elsewhere, or wait for Sales to call.

Most didn't have time to research. They needed a quick way to see, inside the app, whether Edupia suited their child.

03My role

I was Product Owner with a team of 4: 1 Product Designer, 2 Software Engineers and 1 QC Engineer, working with the CPO, Sales, Marketing and Learning Design.

  1. Learned the ground first

    Interviewed parents, sat in on sales calls, and learned from Marketing how leads are handled.

  2. Led design and engineering

    Delivered the trial course, a better onboarding flow and the lead script.

  3. Read the funnel

    Tracked results in Google Analytics and proposed the next improvement.

04The solution

Three calls shaped it.

  1. Let parents try before sales calls.

    They didn't have time to research, and waiting for a call was the only way to learn if Edupia fit.

  2. Keep the trial short, and show what makes Edupia different.

    3 free lessons within 7 days: video lessons with native English teachers, games, and an AI pronunciation check.

  3. Sort leads into groups, and tailor the sales script to each.

    Leads are grouped by their stage in the trial and their lesson results, each with a suggested script, so Sales can sell to every family a little differently.

A parent tries Edupia

Plays on its own. Hover to pause, or pick a step. Sample data.

05What I learned

The trial let parents see the value for themselves. The lead groups let Sales tailor each call to that family. Shaping the whole path, not just the app, is what turned more leads into orders.
