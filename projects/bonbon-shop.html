bonbon shop aims to be the all-in-one app for household businesses: running the shop day to day and staying connected with their distributors, a network NDS Vietnam keeps growing with new distributor and manufacturer partners. This project brought payments into it: shop owners pay their distributor in the app, with a report that proves every payment.

01Outcome

Shop owners now pay inside the app, and the money goes straight to the distributor. Nobody carries cash in between.

3B+VND moved through the system in the pilot
0reconciliation errors
3 mopilot with one distributor in LOF network

02The problem

Distributors sent salespeople to every shop to collect payment. It cost time and money, and it ran on trust.

Cash passed through several hands, and sometimes it never arrived.

They needed two things: payments that go straight to them, and a clean way to check each one.

03My role

I owned it from 0 to 1 as Product Owner, with a team of 5: 1 Product Designer, 3 Software Engineers and 1 QC Engineer.

  1. Did the legal homework first

    Payments are regulated, so I researched the rules and gave the CEO and COO compliant options.

  2. Prototyped the pitch

    A working demo made the idea real for the Business team.

  3. Led the build

    Engineering and two third-party partners (order management system and payment system), from solution design to integration testing.

  4. Launched with Operations

    Trained the team and supported them after go-live.

04The solution

Three calls shaped it.

  1. Pay inside the app, straight to the distributor.

    No collector in the middle: no one to pay, no cash to lose.

  2. Ship the reconciliation report with the payment.

    A way to pay isn't enough. Distributors need to see every payment and its status.

  3. One flow, three surfaces.

    The shop app to pay, the distributor portal to check, the operations portal to watch it all.

05What I learned

Taking cash out of the middle solved half the problem. The other half was proof: a record the distributor could check, payment by payment.
